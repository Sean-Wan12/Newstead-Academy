## Conditions and approvals that affect the forecast

- Total licensed capacity (167) must not be exceeded at any time.
- Each room may use its primary OR an alternate capacity; switching must be reported in CCLS.
- Director approval for mixed-age grouping under s. 8(2) and (3) of O. Reg. 137/15. Limits by rule: up to 3 younger children in a 15-space toddler room, up to 5 in a 24-space preschool room, up to 7 three-year-olds in the 26-space kindergarten room (subject to the kindergarten conditions).
- Shared toddler/preschool/kindergarten playground: 351 sq. m, maximum 63 children at once. Scheduling constraint, not an enrolment limit.
