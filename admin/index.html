        What the forecast assumes

          Staff sign-in and check-in

          • Everyone signs in with their own claude.ai account. The owner shares this admin from its claude.ai page: Contributor for teachers, Editor for supervisors.
          • A supervisor links each teacher's account to their staff record and main room in Logins and configuration. Teachers then see only Classroom check-in for that room, and each check-in records who did it.
          • Owners and Editors see every page and can switch between Richmond Hill and Markham at the top of the screen. Each location keeps its own attendance, waitlist, contacts, billing and calendar.
          • The page decides what each person sees. Anyone the admin is shared with can still read its saved data, so share it only with your staff.
